# Engineering Knowledge Library

A personal library for learning, building, and returning to ideas over time.
Explore independent subjects through courses, practical guides, deep dives,
reference material, and study notes.

The first subject includes the full [Python Backend Engineering curriculum](python-backend-engineering/curriculum-map.md):
10 phases and 20 modules, from Python foundations to production architecture and
senior interviews. Phase 1–2 examples are runnable; later practical projects are
deferred while their lessons, written exercises and assessments are available.

## Choose how to learn

- **Follow a course:** use a subject's learning path to work through a sequence.
- **Solve a problem:** search for a concept, guide, or reference.
- **Practise:** work through exercises and projects, recording evidence at checkpoints.
- **Revisit an idea:** return to your notes and explain the concept in your own words.

Each subject chooses the structure that suits its material. A standalone article
does not need a phase or module, and a reference collection does not need a course.

## Keep knowledge useful

Save the reasoning alongside the answer: assumptions, tradeoffs, examples,
sources, and what you would test next. A small, well-linked explanation is easier
to maintain than several copies of the same material.

Read the [library guide](library-guide.md) for navigation, note-taking conventions,
and how to add a subject.
